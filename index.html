// Independent Researcher · Software Engineer, Amazon Alexa

I am an independent researcher and software engineer at Amazon Alexa. My research focuses on developing agent architectures that combine the flexibility of statistical learning with the interpretability of symbolic reasoning. I aim to optimize how intelligence is distributed across learned parameters, structured knowledge, and external reasoning mechanisms to improve performance, interpretability and domain specialization.

At Amazon Alexa, I currently build conversational voice agents for automotive brands including BMW, BYD, Lamborghini, and Maserati. Previously, at Amazon Smart Vehicles, I worked on operating systems and embedded software for vehicles such as Maserati, Jeep, and Chrysler. Earlier, at the United Nations Human Rights Office, I developed NLP pipelines.
